Vesper the Tidebound

Vesper the Tidebound is the third boss of the Sunken Choir. She guards the Glimmerdeep lantern and attacks with waves of cold water.

Boss overview
Recommended level 42
Weakness Lightning damage
Drops Tide Pearl, Choir Bell

Strategy

Phase 2 begins at 50% HP. Roll backward to dodge the light attack, then press R2 to counter.

Equip the Stormglass Ring before the fight, because her heavy attack drains your stamina.

  • Stay near the pillars during the flood.
  • Use /dodge-roll macros only on PC.
  • Break the four bells to stop her healing song.

Hold jump to reach the upper ledge with the Featherstep Boots.

Patch notes:
Version 1.3 lowered her health.
Version 1.4 fixed the ledge glitch.

Lore

The Sunken Choir was once a cathedral built on the shore of a quiet bay. When the sea rose, the singers refused to leave and kept singing while the water filled the hall. Vesper led the choir, and the old tales say her voice could calm a storm for a whole night. The lantern she guards still holds the last note of that song. Travelers who hear it far from the coast say it sounds like rain on glass. Some scholars in the game believe the lantern is the reason the bay never freezes.
